# PlumeTrace: Brand & Slide Style Guide

This guide establishes the visual identity, presentation design principles, and formatting rules for **PlumeTrace** (Autonomous Digital Twin for River Water Protection).

---

## 1. Core Visual Principles

- **Human & Minimalist:** Clean, spacious, uncluttered layouts with generous whitespace.
- **Ultra-Low Text Density:** Slides must contain **at most ~10% of standard text**—purely punchy bullet points, no full paragraphs or long sentences.
- **No Monospace & No AI Tropes:** Strictly avoid generic AI card designs (such as thick colored left borders or heavy gradient fills). Use clean, subtle borders and soft background tints.
- **No Corner Clutter or Hashtags:** Avoid corner headers, kickers, slide counters, or hashtags on slide canvases. Keep the focus entirely on the core message and the presenter.

---

## 2. Typography

| Role | Font Family | Weight | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **All Headings & Body** | **Calibri** (fallback: `Segoe UI`, system sans-serif) | Bold (700) for Titles, Regular (400) for Bullets | Clean, standard, highly legible, non-technical appearance. |

---

## 3. Color Palette

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Primary: #023047 (Midnight Navy)       Brand: #219ebc (River Cyan)      |
|  Tint:    #8ecae6 (Sky Breeze)          Warning: #ffb703 (Amber Gold)    |
|  Accent:  #fb8500 (Tangerine Orange)    Background: #ffffff (Pure White) |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Color Usage
- **Midnight Navy (`#023047`)**: Primary titles, strong text, and deep contrast elements.
- **River Cyan (`#219ebc`)**: Key brand accents, bullet points, and highlighted terms.
- **Sky Breeze (`#8ecae6`) & Soft Tint (`#f4f9fc`)**: Subtle card backgrounds and delicate dividers.
- **Amber Gold (`#ffb703`) & Tangerine Orange (`#fb8500`)**: Cautionary highlights and critical metrics (e.g. overflow alerts).

---

## 4. Slide Layout & Component Guidelines

### Card & Box Components
- Rounded corners (`border-radius: 8px` to `10px`).
- Subtle neutral border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Very soft background fill (`#f4f9fc` or pure white `#ffffff`).
- Clean bullet list with soft cyan dots or bold hyphens.

### Spoken Word Principle
- The slide provides **visual anchors and key metrics**.
- The presenter delivers the **narrative context, storytelling, and depth**.
