### Slide Content & Copy
- **Kicker**: `CONTEXT & PROBLEM`
- **Slide Title**: **The Pegnitz Paradox: Four Urgent Pressures on an Urban River**
- **Card 1: Public Health & Recreation (The Immediate Hook)**:
  - Fecal contamination directly threatens urban surfers at Nuremberg's **Fuchslochwelle** wave, alongside swimmers and waders in the **Pegnitzauen** parks.
  - Dubbed the **"Durchfall-Welle" (Diarrhea Wave)** in German media (*t-online*, *inFranken*).
  - Swimming is officially banned; existing lab tests take **24–48 hours**, arriving days too late.
- **Card 2: Urban Sewer Infrastructure (CSOs)**:
  - Intense summer convective storms overwhelm municipal sewers, spilling untreated wastewater directly into the river.
  - Modernizing a single retention basin (*RÜB*) costs **€1.5M to €5.0M+**. With ~1,500 km of sewers, cities cannot afford blind infrastructure investments.
- **Card 3: Agriculture & Drinking Water (Knoblauchsland)**:
  - Intensive vegetable farming (~4,000 ha) leaks nitrates through sandy soils into the river and alluvial drinking water aquifers (*Uferfiltrat*).
  - Failure to meet EU standards carries infringement threats reaching **up to €850,000/day**.
- **Card 4: Climate Drought & Ecological Hypoxia**:
  - Summer heatwaves reduce river dilution capacity, driving dissolved oxygen below **3.0 mg/L**.
  - Triggers mass fish kills (e.g. Wöhrder See), requiring boat recovery of floating carcasses.
- **Bottom Callout**:
  - *The Fatal Bottleneck*: Current monitoring relies on monthly manual grab sampling. A 2-hour storm plume is completely invisible to a 30-day testing cycle.

### Spoken Script (Presenter - 75 seconds)
> *"Why the Pegnitz River in Nuremberg? Because it represents a micro-cosmos of every major water challenge facing European cities today.  
> First, **Public Health**: The river hosts the Fuchslochwelle, a famous artificial river wave. Following rainstorms, sewage overflows turn this into what local German media literally dubbed the 'Durchfall-Welle' or Diarrhea Wave. Surfers face severe gastrointestinal infections because municipal bacteria cultures take 24 to 48 hours to process.  
> Second, **Urban Infrastructure**: Nuremberg operates 1,500 kilometers of sewers. Building a single stormwater retention basin costs between 1.5 and 5 million euros. The city cannot afford to guess which outfalls need expansion.  
> Third, **Agriculture**: The adjacent Knoblauchsland region intensively farms vegetables on sandy soils. Nitrogen runoff leaches into alluvial aquifers supplying municipal drinking water.  
> And fourth, **Climate Drought**: Low summer water levels concentrate toxins, dropping oxygen below 3 milligrams per liter and causing severe fish kills.  
> The core bottleneck? Current authorities rely on manual grab samples collected once a month. If a toxic sewer overflow lasts only 3 hours, a monthly sample has a near-zero statistical probability of catching it."*

### Slide Content & Copy
- **Kicker**: `CURRENT BASELINE & BOTTLENECK`
- **Slide Title**: **Current Baseline: What Authorities Measure & The Temporal Flaw**
- **Column 1: The 8 Municipal Parameters Monitored by SUN (*Stadtentwässerung Nürnberg*)**:
  - *Physico-Chemical State*:
    - **Temperature (°C)**: Controls oxygen solubility & biological reaction rates.
    - **pH**: Governs conversion of non-toxic ammonium into lethal un-ionized ammonia ($\text{NH}_3$).
    - **Conductivity ($\mu\text{S/cm}$)**: Dissolved salts from road runoff and industrial effluent.
    - **Turbidity (NTU)**: Suspended particulates from stormwater and sewer discharge.
  - *Ecological & Nutrient Tracers*:
    - **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: River respiration; plummets during organic decomposition.
    - **Ammonium ($\text{NH}_4^+$ / $\text{NH}_3\text{-N}$)**: **Primary sewage tracer**; fish toxicant ($> 0.6\ \text{mg/L}$ breach).
    - **Nitrate ($\text{NO}_3^-$)**: Agricultural fertilizer leaching.
    - **Phosphate ($\text{PO}_4^{3-}$)**: Eutrophication driver causing algal blooms.
- **Column 2: The Fatal Flaw (Frequency & Spatial Blindness)**:
  - **The Temporal Disconnect**: Manual grab sampling (*Schöpfproben*) occurs **once a month (or bi-weekly)**. A storm overflow lasts **2 to 4 hours**.
  - **The Spatial Blind Spot**: Fixed stations only see what drifts over them; they cannot distinguish between outfall pipes 200 meters upstream.
  - **The Conclusion**: Municipalities do not lack chemical knowledge—they lack **dynamic, real-time spatial mobility**.

### Spoken Script (Presenter - 70 seconds)
> *"To solve this problem, we first examined what local authorities already do.  
> In Nuremberg, the municipal service SUN monitors eight standard parameters. They track temperature, pH, conductivity, and turbidity. And they analyze nutrients: Dissolved Oxygen, Ammonium, Nitrate, and Phosphate.  
> These indicators are scientifically sound. Ammonium is the definitive chemical fingerprint of raw sewage. Dissolved Oxygen reveals if the river is suffocating.  
> So why do water crises keep happening?  
> The fatal flaw is **sampling frequency and spatial rigidity**.  
> In Nuremberg, grab samples are taken by hand once every few weeks. Look at the timeline on the right: a convective storm hits on Day 14. For three hours, millions of liters of untreated effluent flood the Pegnitz. By Day 29, when the technician dips a sample bottle, the water is clear again. The statutory violation occurred, the surfers were infected, the fish died, but on paper, the water looked fine.  
> Our challenge is not inventing new chemistry—it is providing the missing spatial and temporal intelligence."*

### Slide Content & Copy
- **Kicker**: `STAKEHOLDERS & SOLUTION VISION`
- **Slide Title**: **Stakeholder Architecture & The Closed-Loop Solution Vision**
- **Formal Challenge Framing**:
  > *"How can digital twinning be applied to autonomous systems in order to address the challenge of detecting and localizing transient Combined Sewer Overflows (CSOs) for the Wasserwirtschaftsamt and regional environmental authorities?"*
- **Primary Stakeholders: Local & Regional Authorities (Targeted Intelligence)**:
  - **WWA Nürnberg (*Wasserwirtschaftsamt*) & LfU**: Shift from monthly grab sampling to real-time mobile gradient tracking and automated statutory alerts in **$< 60\ \text{seconds}$** ($\text{NH}_3\text{-N} > 0.6\ \text{mg/L}$).
  - **SUN (*Stadtentwässerung Nürnberg*)**: Shift from internal overflow weir counters to quantitative **3D In-River Pollution Heatmaps** in Cesium/LOD2, guiding multi-million euro retention investments.
  - **The Continuous Spatial Profiler**: Beyond sudden spills, repeated patrols uncover **chronic low-level pipe seepage** and cracked sewer joints that static sensors never catch.
- **Secondary Stakeholders: Citizens & Recreators (Immediate Protection)**:
  - **Fuchslochwelle Surfers & Pegnitzauen Park Visitors**:
  - Intuitive **"Traffic Light" Web App**:
    - 🟢 **Green (Safe)**: Meets recreational water criteria.
    - 🟡 **Yellow (Caution)**: Marginal conditions / elevated post-storm runoff.
    - 🔴 **Red (Hazard)**: Active sewage spill – **Do not enter water today!**
- **The Core Solution Concept: 4D Closed-Loop Adaptive Retasking**:
  - The autonomous platform streams real-time proxy data ($\text{NH}_3\text{-N}$, $\text{DO}$) every 30 seconds.
  - The Digital Twin actively re-computes where the robot must sample next based on:
    1. **Data Age ($\tau$)**: Exploring unvisited zones.
    2. **Chemical Gradient ($\nabla C$)**: Homing upstream to the active pipe.
    3. **Rain Radar ($W$)**: Intercepting storm cell runoff.
    4. **Historical Baselines ($H$)**: Filtering normal variations.

### Spoken Script (Presenter - 75 seconds)
> *"This leads directly to our formal challenge definition:  
> **'How can digital twinning be applied to autonomous systems in order to address the challenge of detecting and localizing transient Combined Sewer Overflows for regional water authorities?'**  
> We serve two distinct stakeholder tiers:  
> For **Local Authorities**—the Wasserwirtschaftsamt, city sewer utility SUN, and public health departments—we provide deep infrastructure intelligence. Instead of counting weir overflows inside pipes, our robot acts as a **continuous spatial profiler**. It cruises the river over time, uncovering chronic, low-level seepage from cracked pipes before catastrophic failure occurs, and delivers 3D pollution heatmaps to guide multi-million euro infrastructure investments.  
> For **Citizens and Surfers**, we eliminate confusing chemical graphs. We translate real-time data into a simple **Traffic Light mobile app**: Green means enjoy the wave, Red means an active sewer plume was detected upstream, stay out of the water today.  
> And how does the system operate?  
> Through an **autonomous closed loop**. Rather than driving a rigid, pre-planned path, our Digital Twin ingests telemetry every 30 seconds and computes an optimal 4D objective function—balancing data freshness, chemical gradients, weather radar, and historical baselines to vector the robot exactly where it is needed most."*
