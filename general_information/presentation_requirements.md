# Presentation & Defence Requirements

Guidelines, section-by-section requirements, and evaluation criteria for the final defence presentation.

---

## Presentation Structure & Slide Limits

| Section # | Section Name | Max Slides | Target Audience | Key Focus Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Title Slide** | 1 | General | Team name, contributing members |
| **2** | **Context and Challenge** | 3 | Stakeholders | Relevant SDG context, stakeholder needs, formal challenge framing |
| **3** | **Technical Solution** | 3 | Stakeholders & General Tech | Architecture overview, key design decisions, DT rationale |
| **4** | **Proof of Concept (PoC)** | 4 | Stakeholders & General Tech | Scope delineation (full vs PoC), DTAS criteria, revised diagrams |
| **5** | **Demonstration** | Video / Slides | Technical Experts (Stakeholder relevant) | Live/recorded demo, bidirectional comms, state sync, env interaction |
| **6** | **Evaluation & Reflection** | Flexible | Technical Experts | Delivered vs intended scope, testing, risks, limitations, future work |

---

## Detailed Section Breakdown

### 1. Title Slide (Max 1 slide)
- Team name.
- Contributing members.

### 2. Context and Challenge (Max 3 slides)
- **Target Audience**: Stakeholders.
- **Relevant SDG Context**: United Nations Sustainable Development Goals addressed by the project.
- **Stakeholders and Needs**: Identification of primary and secondary stakeholders and their concrete pain points.
- **Challenge Definition**: Must be explicitly formulated as:
  > *"How can digital twinning be applied to autonomous systems in order to address the `<challenge(s)>` of `<stakeholder(s)>`?"*

### 3. Technical Solution (Max 3 slides)
- **Target Audience**: Stakeholders and those with general technical knowledge.
- **Proposed Technical Solution Overview**:
  - High-level system context and architecture diagrams.
- **Key Design Decisions**:
  - Explicit reference to DTAS (Digital Twinning for Autonomous Systems) criteria.
- **Rationale for Digital Twin Approach**:
  - Justification for why digital twinning is the appropriate paradigm over alternative approaches.

### 4. Proof of Concept (PoC) (Max 4 slides)
- **Target Audience**: Stakeholders and those with general technical knowledge.
- **Delineation**: Clear boundary between the overarching full technical solution vs. the specific PoC implemented within the project scope.
- **Functional and Non-Functional Scope**:
  - Mapped against DTAS criteria.
- **Architecture Diagrams**:
  - Revised context / architecture diagrams specifically reflecting what is within the PoC.
- **Relationship**:
  - How the PoC validates and fits into the broader long-term technical solution.

### 5. Demonstration (Video / Slides as needed)
- **Target Audience**: Technical experts (while keeping stakeholder relevance evident).
- **Core DTAS Evidence Required**:
  1. **Bidirectional Communication**: Data flowing between physical/autonomous system and digital twin.
  2. **State Synchronisation**: Digital twin accurately reflecting and updating the physical state in real time / timely manner.
  3. **Environmental Interaction**: Twin and physical asset reacting to, modeling, or influencing environmental conditions.

### 6. Evaluation and Technical Reflection
- **Target Audience**: Technical experts.
- **Functionality Categorization Matrix**:
  - **Intended Functionality**: What was planned originally.
  - **Delivered Functionality**: Fully implemented and validated features.
  - **Partially Implemented Functionality**: Features in-progress or with partial fidelity.
  - **Functionality Not Implemented**: Omitted or postponed capabilities.
- **Full Evaluation & Reflection Components**:
  - **Testing and Calibration**: Methodologies and calibration activities performed.
  - **Results and Evidence**: Data collected during testing.
  - **Strengths and Limitations**: Objective analysis of implementation performance.
  - **Risks and Assumptions**: Analysis of risks/assumptions that influenced outcomes, including reflection on the adequacy of the original project risk assessment.
  - **Technical Reflections**: Key engineering insights gained from implementation.
  - **Future Development Opportunities**: Roadmap for production deployment or future iterations.
